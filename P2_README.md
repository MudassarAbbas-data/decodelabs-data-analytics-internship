## Key Findings
- **Top City:** Lahore generates highest total revenue (PKR 27,703)
- **Top Category:** Clothing leads in total revenue
- **Key Segment:** Age group 26-30 is the largest customer group
- **Outliers:** 5 detected using IQR method (high & low value customers)
- **Retention:** Majority of customers are Active
