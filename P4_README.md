## Project Overview
Built a comprehensive 8-chart visualization dashboard using Matplotlib presenting complete business intelligence from the Customer Sales dataset in a stakeholder-ready format.

## Charts Built
| Chart | Type | Insight |
|-------|------|---------|
| Chart 1 | Bar Chart | Revenue by Product Category |
| Chart 2 | Line Chart | Monthly Revenue Trend |
| Chart 3 | Pie Chart | Customer Distribution by City |
| Chart 4 | Scatter Plot | Age vs Revenue Relationship |
| Chart 5 | Grouped Bar | Revenue by City & Category |
| Chart 6 | Box Plot | Revenue Spread by Category |
| Chart 7 | Horizontal Bar | Active vs Inactive by City |
| Dashboard | Multi-panel | Full Business Overview + KPIs |

## Key Findings
- Clothing leads in total revenue across all cities
- Lahore is the strongest market
- 72% of customers are Active (strong retention)
- Age has no strong impact on spending behavior
- Food category shows highest revenue variability
